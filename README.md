## About

Principal Software Engineer with a proven track record of designing, building, and maintaining production-grade web applications and enterprise business systems. I specialise in backend architecture, API integration, and travel technology, with a focus on understanding systems deeply and delivering solutions that remain reliable in real-world production environments.

Alongside my engineering work, I lead technical direction, mentor developers, conduct code reviews, and guide architecture and planning decisions. I also maintain an active interest in applied artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.

## Core Expertise

- **Multi-Channel Airline Distribution:** Integrate and operate across major distribution channels, including Travelport, Amadeus, and Sabre GDS platforms, NDC, direct airline APIs, and supplier partner portals, unifying fragmented content into a single booking experience.

- **Automation for Non-API Suppliers:** Architect resilient Playwright-based automation that brings API-less supplier portals into automated booking workflows, handling OTP-based authentication, persistent session lifecycles, and dynamic form interactions with production-grade reliability.

- **Scalable Integration Architecture:** Design adapter-based integration layers that abstract supplier-specific protocols behind a unified, extensible interface, reducing the cost of onboarding new suppliers and isolating the core platform from third-party changes.

- **High-Performance Search & Booking:** Engineer asynchronous processing pipelines using queues, background workers, and multi-layer caching to deliver low-latency search and fault-tolerant booking under concurrent, multi-supplier load.

- **Technical Leadership:** Set technical direction, mentor engineers, lead code reviews, and drive architecture and delivery planning across B2B travel and business platforms.
